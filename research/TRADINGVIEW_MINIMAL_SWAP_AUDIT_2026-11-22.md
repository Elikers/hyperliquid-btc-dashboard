# TradingView indicator-source audit and minimal-swap decision — 2026-11-22

## Scope and evidence standard
This is a **curated, not exhaustive**, audit of public TradingView strategy listings and publicly retrievable Pine sources. There are far too many scripts to audit "all" TradingView indicators. **Open-source does not imply profitable**; author-provided strategy tester returns are claims, not independent evidence. TradingView's own broker emulator documentation warns of historical fill assumptions, slippage, lookahead/repainting, selection bias, and overfitting: https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/strategies/ and https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000786181-broker-emulator/ .

The TradingView public listing pages disclosed methodology and open-source status, but their source-editor contents were not directly retrievable through the available public HTML view. Therefore the audit distinguishes **inspected individual Pine source** (public GitHub files) from **inspected TradingView public script descriptions**; it does not assert line-level verification of TradingView-hosted source where inaccessible. Third-party implementation text is **not copied** into this repo.

## Viprasol Classic baseline: do not change
The actual BTC research engine uses EMA9/21 **cross** as an unconditional candidate event gate, with min 5/7 votes from VWAP, RSI(14), MACD, EMA direction, ADX(14)>25, volume versus SMA20, and a confirming 15m RSI. Other gates: ADR14, weekend/session; ATR14*1.5 stop with fixed 1R/2R/3R levels and later-bar breakeven after TP1. The current research simulation assumes ideal **signal candle close** entry, conservative future OHLC stop-first outcomes, and hypothetical 0/7/15bps one-way cost sensitivity. TradingView original Pine calculations/ATR parity are unverified, and no real fills exist.

Past evidence:
- ~27-day recent historical BTC sample: 13/7/7 15m/30m/1h hypothetical candidates. 16 SL + 6 BE + 5 TP3. Approx net R at 7bps: -3.074/-3.246/+0.193 per timeframe (not portfolio PnL).
- CHOP<=61.8 experimental **additional** filter hurt 15m by 2.529R, and appeared to help 30m/1h by skipping only three losing trades on the original sample.
- Completely nonoverlapping earlier historical holdout (7/2/1 hypothetical trades) **skipped zero entries** at that CHOP cutoff. No replicated advantage. At 15bps, its 15m grossly positive trades summed to **-0.555R** after costs.

## Minimal change decision

**First fix research validity, then consider ONE entry trigger swap.**

1. **Research execution defect (confirmed from our code):** Signal-bar **close** price is used as guaranteed entry execution. For a candle-close signal, an order might fill at a later price (e.g., next candle open plus slippage), changing stop distance, R multiples and outcomes. Add a **separate conservative next-bar-open fill stress test** while leaving the exact Classic entry/ATR/TP levels untouched in the baseline. The current 0/7/15bps sensitivity only shifts monetary results; it does not re-evaluate stops and limits at changed fill prices. Similarly audit stop-first versus lower-timeframe order sequence; do not assert instant execution or exact PnL until fixed.
2. **One research entry-swap candidate:** Replace **EMA9/21 crossover as the *event trigger*** (not add another one of seven votes) with a **confirmed swing-level liquidity sweep + closing reclaim**. Compute only on closed bars; pivot confirmation available only after right-hand bars have actually arrived. Keep original seven scoring indicators, ADR/session/weekend constraints and original ATR exits **constant initially** to isolate the entry effect. Critically, the fixed Classic EMA confluence factor is not secretly changed, even when trigger itself changes; record this deliberate Lab-only difference.
3. **Do not replace ADX/RSI/MACD with another lagging indicator yet.** Their apparent correlation is a mathematical concern, not individually demonstrated harmful attribution. Perform factor ablation with holdout data only if enough unique entries exist; do not fit on 27+10 trades.
4. **No CHOP promotion:** earlier real nonoverlapping holdout showed zero improvement; don't tune 61.8 or selectively activate only timeframes that looked good.
5. **No claims of 'proven profitable indicators'** without authenticated source/settings, trade-by-trade history on target exchange, independent chronological validation, measured commission/slippage, confidence intervals and prospective paper evidence.

**Promotion gate:** Not until independent source Pine parity, valid closed-bar no-leak simulation, more independent market regimes and actual forward paper trade lifecycle outcomes with net expectancy after costs. Keep Viprasol Classic/dashboard unchanged, `discordEligible=false`, `automaticTrading=false`. External Pine licenses/TradingView House Rules apply; do not transplant source code into our public repository without permission.

### Why no code was swapped here
The user requested selecting the **one** weakness based on **proven results**. The identified external listings don't supply independent target-market proof, so immediately swapping Classic (or calling the candidate profitable) would violate the project's evidence gate. The new work is a source-backed diagnosis and an explicit one-swap experimental specification, not an activated or proven Hybrid.
